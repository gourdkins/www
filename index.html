About Us

Our family has been harvesting these unique dried gourds and pumpkins for over 20 years. Each autumn, we look forward to seeing the new and some old familiar faces that have evolved for us to share with our current and new friends. Our goal each harvest is to find homes for these personalities and create long time friends that come back to add to their family each year. Keep them, share them and enjoy them as they move on from their home with us in Parker, CO to your homes all across the world.

You can see the current harvest in person every Sunday in October at the Parker, CO Farmers Market. For those of you out of state, please watch for updated pictures as we harvest new gourds throughout the month of October.
